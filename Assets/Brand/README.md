# BookSlide app icon

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The opaque master is used by iPhone,
iPad and Watch; the transparent, rounded master is used by Mac. Run
`python3 scripts/assets/generate_icons.py` to export Xcode sizes with `sips`.

The artwork is an ivory open book with a lifted page on deep forest green.
The shape remains recognizable at small sizes, without lettering or badges.
Asset catalog packaging follows [Apple's app-icon documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-your-app-icon).

## Master prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production square app icon artwork for BookSlide, a refined EPUB reading app for Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple Watch. Create one exceptionally clear, premium and understated icon: an elegant open book made of two broad warm ivory paper shapes, a subtle central spine and one gracefully lifted page suggesting effortless page movement. Strong iconic silhouette, excellent recognition at tiny watch size. Deep forest green background inspired by a quiet reading room; delicate tonal depth, soft restrained paper relief, precise edges, sophisticated editorial design. Center the book, occupy about 66 percent of canvas width and 56 percent height. The open book must unmistakably read as a book, not wings or a bird. Single icon only, straight-on view, no mockup, no typography, no letters, no extra badge, no arrows, no border. Full-bleed opaque square background to all four corners, no rounded outer corners, no external shadow or surrounding margin; the operating system will mask the icon. Output a high resolution square image.

## Mac edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. This is the final BookSlide icon artwork. Make its macOS app-icon version. Preserve the exact open ivory book, forest-green background, page curl, lighting and composition within the icon. Change ONLY the outer silhouette and surrounding canvas: place the entire existing square artwork inside one centered macOS-style continuous rounded-square silhouette occupying about 82% of a square canvas, with smoothly rounded corners and a very subtle soft shadow. The outer area beyond this rounded square must be truly transparent, including all four corners. Keep all the book inside the rounded square. One icon, no extra objects, no text, no mockup, square high-resolution output.
